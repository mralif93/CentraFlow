Centralized Single Sign-Out (SLO)

When an administrator or employee signs out of any sub-system portal, CentraFlow guarantees comprehensive session destruction across both the local client cookie and the master IdP session.

1. Client Session Destroyed: Session::flush()
2. OAuth Token Revocation: Token::where('user_id', ...)->revoke()
3. Master Session Clear: GET http://localhost:8004/logout
4. Cookie Collision Shield: centraflow_session ≠ pulsehr_session
